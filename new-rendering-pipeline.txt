
!!! WILL REQUIRE COMPLETE REWORK OF ENTITY, DRAWABLE, CAMERA, RENDERING PIPELINE AND WINDOWING SYSTEM !!!

Scene:
- contains a list of drawables, camera's, entities etc

    Rendering:
        renders each camera

Drawables:
    On Enable:
        registers itself to the scene as a drawable

    Rendering:
        renders itself using the rendering pipeline on the GPU

Camera:
- contains a buffer to render on (e.g. backbuffer, filestream)

    On Enable:
        registers itself to the scene as a camera

    Rendering:
        renders all drawables in the Scene to the buffer
